About Me

One person, the whole setup.

I’m a Digital Marketing Expert based in Butuan City, Philippines, with two years of experience supporting small businesses and agencies with content creation, digital marketing, and day-to-day marketing operations. With an English degree background, I bring strong communication skills, attention to detail, and a creative approach to helping brands improve their online presence.

Curious and proactive. I don’t just focus on completing tasks; I take the time to understand the goals behind them, identify areas for improvement, and find more efficient ways to support business growth.

My experience covers marketing tasks, including social media management, content creation, graphic design, video editing, SEO (on-page) support, website/funnel creation and updates, and paid ads assistance. I help businesses stay organized and consistent by providing dependable support across different areas of their marketing.

My goal is to become a reliable extension of your team — helping you execute ideas, improve processes, and free up your time so you can focus on growing your business.
